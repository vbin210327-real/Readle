句读院隐私政策

生效日期 2026 年 11 月 19 日

句读院无需注册账号。我们不在 App 中投放广告，也不使用跨 App 跟踪或第三方分析服务。

书籍、头像与阅读记录

导入或通过系统分享菜单添加的书籍、封面、阅读位置、阅读统计、译文、查词记录和听书音频由 App 在设备上保存。你从系统相册选择的头像及设置的昵称也保存在设备上。分享扩展会先在 App 与扩展共用的本地容器中暂存文件，导入后移除暂存副本。我们不接收这些资料。

翻译与朗读

翻译、查词和朗读在设备上运行。普通版的翻译次数也在设备上记录。你确认下载额外离线声音时，App 会从 Hugging Face 获取声音文件。下载会产生普通网络请求，阅读文本和书籍不会随请求发送。

购买与恢复

Apple 处理付款、订阅和恢复购买。句读院通过 StoreKit 获取购买记录并在设备上保存用于验证高级版的状态。我们不会获取你的银行卡号或 Apple 账户密码。

保留与删除

你可以在书架删除书籍及其阅读位置、书内译文和音频，在「我的」移除头像或清空昵称。跨书籍缓存的查词结果保留在本机供重复使用。删除 App 会清除设备上的 App 本地数据，iOS 备份依你的系统设置处理。Apple 的购买记录由 Apple 管理。

联系

如有隐私问题，可通过句读院项目支持页面联系开发者。请勿在公开留言中提供个人信息。本网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问可能由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理。

Readle Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Readle requires no account and uses no in-app advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics.

Books, profile, and reading data

Readle stores imported books, covers, reading positions and statistics, translations, lookups, and listening audio on your device. Your selected profile photo and nickname are also stored on your device. The share extension holds files temporarily in a local container shared with the app and removes the staged copy after import. We do not receive this material.

Translation and speech

Translation, lookups, and speech run on your device. The free translation allowance is recorded locally. If you approve an optional offline voice download, the app fetches the files from Hugging Face. The download involves a normal network request and sends no reading text or book files.

Purchases and restoration

Apple processes payments, subscriptions, and restored purchases. Readle uses StoreKit purchase records and caches membership status on your device. We do not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password.

Retention and deletion

Delete a book from the shelf to remove its reading position, book-specific translations, and audio. Shared lookup results are cached locally for reuse. In My Profile, you can remove your photo or clear your nickname. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Your system settings govern iOS backups, and Apple manages App Store purchase records.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Readle support page. Keep personal information out of public posts. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process visit data under its privacy statement.
