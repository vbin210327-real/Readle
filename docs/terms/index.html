高级版与购买

高级版提供 3D 书架、影院模式、听书及更高的翻译和导入权限。你可通过 App Store 购买月度或年度订阅，也可以一次性购买终身高级版。结账时，App Store 会显示适用的价格、订阅周期及可用试用优惠。

月度和年度订阅会按购买时显示的条款自动续订，直至取消。试用到期后会按确认的价格转为付费订阅。要避免下一次扣费，请在当前周期或试用结束至少 24 小时前通过 Apple 账户的订阅设置取消。终身高级版为一次性购买。

订阅到期后，已有的书籍仍可在普通版阅读，高级功能随订阅结束而锁定。使用购买时的 Apple 账户可在 App 中恢复符合条件的购买。付款及退款由 Apple 处理。管理或取消订阅 · 申请退款

Pro plans and purchases

Readle Pro includes the 3D bookshelf, Theater Mode, listening, and higher translation and import allowances. You can purchase a monthly or yearly subscription or make a one-time lifetime purchase through the App Store. At checkout, the App Store shows the applicable price, billing interval, and any trial available to you.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically under the terms shown at purchase until canceled. A trial converts to a paid subscription at the confirmed price when it ends. To avoid the next charge, cancel from your Apple Account subscription settings at least 24 hours before the current period or trial ends. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase.

When your subscription ends, your existing books remain readable on the free tier, and Pro features lock. Use the Apple Account you purchased with to restore eligible purchases in Readle. Apple handles payments and refunds. Manage or cancel a subscription · Request a refund
