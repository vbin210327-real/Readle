Readle's word dictionary, words.tsv, combines and adapts:

- Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), by its contributors, as extracted by kaikki.org / wiktextract
  (Tatu Ylonen). Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.
- CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org), published by MDBG and its contributors; CEDICT copyright 1997, 1998
  Paul Andrew Denisowski. Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.
- Chinese Open Wordnet 1.1 (https://bond-lab.github.io/cow), copyright 2013-2026 Francis Bond, Shan Wang
  and Luis Morgado da Costa.
- WordNet 3.0, copyright 2006 by Princeton University.
- Wikidata names (https://www.wikidata.org). CC0.

Changes: only English words with Chinese meanings were kept, traditional characters were turned into
simplified ones, the sources were merged and shortened, and some small words were written anew
(scripts/build-word-dictionary.py).
